# Kino v2 — the engines behind the 15 tester points (2026-10-24)

The owner read 40 hours of chats as a stranger and listed 15 things a tester notices. Grouped by ROOT CAUSE they are
five problems, not fifteen. A patch per symptom is what produced today's 60 regexes. Below: one engine per cause, what
it owns, where it plugs in, and how it is measured. Order = build order.

| # | tester points | cause | engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 pairs within a minute, 6 self-corrections, 7 repeated sermon, 8 same closing formula, 15 same text in two chats | **many writers, no editor**: quick turn, build turn, claim auditor, inner voice, events all append bubbles independently | **Voice** (`kino_voice.py`, LIVE 2026-10-24): one outbox per chat, one bubble per member turn, corrections edit the bubble in place, cross-chat dedupe, "already told" memory |
| 2 | 1 "je lance" and nothing runs, 2 raw errors, 4 promises never kept, 13 confused by its own rule | **words and actions are two systems**: the model narrates an action; code runs it elsewhere; failures leak as exception names | **Deed** (`kino_deed.py`): a say may only claim a deed that exists as a job (queued/running/done/failed); job failures are retried once and told in plain words; capability registry decides what can be promised |
| 3 | 3 wrong alarming facts (ghost vs real), 14 numbers wobble | **state told from memory**: where a robot runs, which target, which test is in the queue are regenerated by the model from prose | **Ledger facts** (`kino_facts.py`): a structured STATE block (robot → where it runs, target, test state) keyed and versioned; the auditor checks state claims against it, and numbers once said are pinned |
| 4 | 9 answers a different question, 10 tone misses, 11 blames the audio, 12 language drift | **no listening step**: the quick turn generates before it has decided what was asked, in which language, under which member constraint | **Ear** (`kino_ear.py`): a cheap structured read of the member's message (asked, language, is-short, refers-to), hard member constraints ("no money" → never suggest spending), audio-cutoff policy |
| 5 | 4 (promised what the tool cannot do), part of 1 | **no self-knowledge of tools** | **Capability registry** (inside Deed): what each analysis tool can and cannot measure, in the prompt and as a gate on promises |

Points 6/7/8 also lean on the exam (`kino_exam.py`) to prove the change, not on a new regex.

## Engine 1 — Voice (one voice, one bubble) — LIVE 2026-10-24

Owns every `ai` message: `_chat_msg` hands each one to `kino_voice.post(c, m)` before it is appended.

- **Merge window.** A build/follow reply arriving within 120 s of a quick reply to the same member message becomes ONE
  bubble: a hedge ("à vue de nez", "je regarde") is replaced by the result, a real sentence keeps the result as its second
  paragraph. The page repaints the edited bubble (it polls and repaints the whole list).
- **Correction = edit, never a new bubble.** The claim auditor's or fact checker's correction replaces the sentence it
  contradicts inside the original bubble (the auditor passes the unsupported claims; best word overlap picks the
  sentence) and marks it `fixed` (the page shows "· corrigé"). The member never reads "Petite nuance", "je me corrige",
  "fausse alerte". A bubble already fixed is not fixed twice. If the member replied since, or the bubble is older than
  30 min, the correction stands as a plain new bubble.
- **Cross-chat dedupe.** An own message with ≥ 0.8 word overlap with a message sent to another chat of the same member
  in the last 30 min is dropped.
- **Told memory** `robots/kino_told.json`: the sermon lines ("un test passé n'est pas une promesse", "3-4 semaines de
  fantôme", "c'est ta décision", "ton compte n'est pas touché", "rien ne part seul", "intuition, pas mesuré", "avec ton
  mot de passe") are counted per member. Said twice in 7 days → the sentence is removed from the next say unless the
  member's message asked about it. The slow prompt carries "ALREADY SAID ENOUGH: …".
- **Closer.** "Mon avis : / Honnêtement, / Franchement," opening the last sentence of three messages in a row: the
  opener goes, the content stays.
- **Budget.** At most 3 own bubbles (events) per chat per hour with no member reply in between.
- Measured in the scorecard: `voice` = {merge, fix, dup, told, closer, budget} per day and `bubbles_per_turn`
  (target 1.0). Replay on the 22-23 October chats: 378 bubbles → 342; 10 merges, 6 corrections folded in place,
  10 cross-chat duplicates dropped, 23 sermon sentences cut (ghost weeks 7, "pas mesuré" 7, "ta décision" 5…),
  3 closers cut, 10 own bubbles over budget.

## Engine 2 — Deed (a word for an action is a job)

- **Job object** `{id, kind: test|build|luck|measure|ghost, cid, state: queued|running|done|failed, t, due, err_plain}`
  in `robots/kino_deeds.json`. Created by code the moment an action is started (chat_test, _chat_build, luck queue,
  measure tool), never by text.
- **Binding.** A say containing an action verb for self (je lance / c'est parti / je le construis / je relance /
  launching) must name a job created in this turn or in the last 120 s for this chat. No job → the sentence is replaced
  by the job-less truth from a small template set, in the member's language: "Je ne l'ai pas lancé : il manque X" (from
  the job's err_plain) or the action IS started now (act_test) when it is possible. The commitment ledger
  (`kino_commit.py`) keeps the promise accounting; Deed is the gate at send time.
- **Failures.** A job that fails is retried once after 20 s. Only then the member hears one plain sentence from the
  error catalogue (file lock → "l'app était occupée, je réessaie dans une minute"; missing robot → "il n'y a pas de robot
  dans cette conversation"). The exception name never reaches the chat; the traceback goes to `chat_errors.log`.
- **Capability registry** `robots/kino_can.json`: each analysis tool with `can` / `cannot` sentences (e.g. breakdown:
  can slice an IDEA by hour/weekday; cannot slice the Owl's own trades by hour). Printed in the prompt ("WHAT YOU CAN
  MEASURE HERE"); the commit extractor tags a promise `impossible` when it matches a `cannot` → the say is rewritten to
  "je ne peux pas le mesurer ici" on the first attempt, not the fourth.
- Measured: says with an unbound action verb (target 0), raw error bubbles (0), promises broken / (kept+broken).

## Engine 3 — Ledger facts (state is data)

- A **STATE block** built by code each turn: for each robot of the member: name, where it runs (`real account` /
  `ghost` / `nowhere`), since when, target, test state (none / queued position / running n/100 / done verdict), last
  result's key numbers. Versioned (`state_v`); the same text goes to the quick turn, the build turn and the auditor.
- The auditor (`_on_claim`) gets a third checklist: **placement claims** ("tourne sur ton compte", "en fantôme") and
  **parameter claims** (target, stop, hours) must match the STATE block. A mismatch is corrected in place (Voice).
- **Pinned numbers.** Each number Kino says with a unit is stored `{value, unit, about, t}` per chat; a later say giving a
  different value for the same `about` within 24 h without a new result card is flagged and corrected before sending.

## Engine 4 — Ear (listen before speaking)

- One small structured call per member message (Haiku, ~$0.001): `{asked: "open_trade_pnl"|"account_balance"|
  "robot_state"|"opinion"|"smalltalk"|…, question: bool, lang: fr|en|mixed, short: bool, refers_to: "own_prev"|"their_prev"|
  null, constraint_hit: ["no_money", …]}`. Stored on the message as `ear`.
- **Answer-the-ask check.** If `asked` names a quantity the facts hold (open trade P&L, balance, week), the quick say
  must contain that number or the card; otherwise the number is injected as the first sentence.
- **Reply language = the message's language** when `lang` is unambiguous and the message has ≥ 4 words; else the chat's
  language. Own messages follow the member's dominant language. No more flapping on "Test of random".
- **Member constraints** from `kino_person`/feedback (no money for outings, hates repetition, small-and-regular) become a
  short DON'T list in the fast prompt and an auditor rule; "resto/veste" to a member flagged `no_money` is rewritten.
- **Audio cut-offs:** "ta phrase s'est coupée" allowed once per hour per chat; otherwise answer the most likely reading
  and say which one was taken.

## What is retired when each engine lands

- Voice: the hedge regex in the quick put (kept for now: it avoids posting the hedge at all), `_LAST_PROMISE_FIRE`
  dedupe, `_once` for sys lines, the "says again what it just said" check, topic_check's cross-chat part, the closer
  regex in tic_check.
- Deed: the `je lance … test` regex → act, `_test_retry`, the "Oups (%s)" formatter.
- Facts: the per-call `nest` lists in `_on_claim`; the "told" fields scattered in material/feedback.
- Ear: `member_lang` / `member_lang2` heuristics, the "compte/solde → account card" regex, the "phrase s'est coupée" habit.

## Proof

Each engine ships with: its scorecard counters, a replay of the 22-23 October chats through the new gate (how many of the
15 symptoms it would have stopped), and the exam (≥ baseline 22/24). Live on the owner's account the same day (standing
rule), results compared on the next day's scorecard.

"files outside the allowed list appeared" and `revert()` deleted them (second time: see memory

ALLOW list and moves new files to `lab/revert_trash/<stamp>/` instead of deleting them; work outside the perimeter is
named in the build log and left alone.
