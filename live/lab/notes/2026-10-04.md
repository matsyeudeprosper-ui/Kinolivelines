## English

**Tonight: aim for as much as we risk and let the robot rest on Sunday — better on both halves, with AND without a daily cap. A first.**

Verdicts, last 42 days: 37 ideas, none better on both halves, 10 a little better, 27 no. Aim 0.9 + four catch-up trades (yesterday): a little better, +$41 without a cap, -$4 with one but a smaller hole, +$164 over 69 days. Aim 1.0 alone: no with a cap, seventh night.

My 20 tries (robot's safety nets, accounts with and without a cap): aim only for what is missing to reach the day's cap: no at every setting (-$21 to -$31). Aim 1.0 without Sunday: +$46 and +$16, holes of 34 instead of 50 and 22 instead of 34. A fourth catch-up adds $4, a fifth nothing. The neighbouring 0.95 says no with a cap: the money jumps, the hole always shrinks. Over 69 days: a little better.

What I believe:
- Aim 1.0 without Sunday helps both kinds of account: NEW.
- Changes of direction in a nervous market earn nothing: NEW.
- Aim only for what is missing to reach the cap: REJECTED.
- Stake less when nervous: STRONGER, as a safety belt.
- Sunday: STRONGER.
- A fourth catch-up: WEAKER.

The real trades (42): 52% won, +$0.30; counted in "times what we risk", a small loss that the bigger stakes hide. Changes of direction: 9 won of 21, 4 of 13 in a nervous market; same way: 12 of 18. The test sees the same. The weekend's two big losses are ordinary stops with a bigger stake. Too few trades.
New pile: changes of direction in a nervous market.

I propose: aim 1.0 without Sunday; with four catch-ups; aim 1.0 and four catch-ups, Sunday included; without chasing 700 points; out of the box: aim 1.0, no Sunday, a quarter less when nervous (hole halved).

I ask: stake less on a change of direction when the market is nervous.
